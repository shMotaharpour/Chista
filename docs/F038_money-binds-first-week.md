# F038 — Money binds in the first week

**Summary (<=50 words):** startingMoney = 3,000, and every purchase refuses
silently when the purse is short. A real plan ends day 1 with 13 coins and
only breaks out around day 10 — the first week is where money binds.

## Finding

- `startingMoney = 3,000`.
- Every purchase refuses in silence when the purse is short.
- The first week is where money binds: a real plan ends day 1 with
  **13 coins**, and only breaks out around day 10.

*Source: "Kaggriculture — the rules, as we have established them" research
document, section 4 (Money).*
