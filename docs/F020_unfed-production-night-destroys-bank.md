# F020 — Unfed production night destroys the bank

**Summary (<=50 words):** An unfed production night destroys the care bank: the bonus is popped only when fed, but the counter is zeroed either way — a bank of three becomes nothing. An unfed ordinary night leaves the bank untouched.

## Finding

- An unfed production night destroys the bank: the bonus is popped only when fed, but the counter is zeroed either way — a bank of three walks into a hungry production night and comes out as nothing.
- An unfed *ordinary* night leaves the bank alone.

*Source: "Crops and animals — every rule, numbered" research document.*
