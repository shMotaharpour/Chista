# F021 — Care bank lumps before first yield

**Summary (<=50 words):** The care bank accumulates through the wait before the first yield and pays out in one lump: fed and cared from the placement day, a goose reaches its first production night with a bank of 3 and collects 1 + 3 = 4 — exactly its max_held.
