# F014 — Ongoing measured calendars

**Summary (<=50 words):** Measured, watered daily: TOMATO yields its first unit on night 7, then 8, 9, 10, and is a weed by night 12; STRAWBERRY yields on nights 9, 11, 13, 15 and dies from day 17.
