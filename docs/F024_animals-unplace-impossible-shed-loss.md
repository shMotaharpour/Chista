# F024 — Animals cannot be unplaced, can be lost in the shed

**Summary (<=50 words):** A placed animal cannot be taken back: DIG returns early on a tile holding one, and only escape empties the structure. An animal still in the shed at nightfall can be destroyed by the 100-unit shed overflow like any other good.

## Finding

- A placed animal cannot be taken back: DIG returns early on a tile that holds one. Only escape empties the structure.
- An animal still in the shed at nightfall can be destroyed by the shed's 100-unit overflow, like any other good.

*Source: "Crops and animals — every rule, numbered" research document, project [AgriOracle](https://github.com/shMotaharpour/AgriOracle).*
