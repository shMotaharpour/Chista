# F036 — The price ladder is coarse; sell pressure bites only in shallow markets

**Summary (<=50 words):** The price ladder is coarse — about 25 wheat units
per coin — so a handful of extra units can tip a fifty-unit basket down a
step, and one more wheat tile can be worth less than nothing. Sell pressure
bites only in shallow markets.

## Finding

- The ladder is coarse: about 25 units of wheat to the coin. A handful of
  extra units can tip a fifty-unit basket down a step, which is why one more
  wheat tile can be worth *less than nothing*.
- Sell pressure only bites in shallow markets; the deep ones do not move.

*Source: "Kaggriculture — the rules, as we have established them" research
document, section 3 (Prices).*
